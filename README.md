# My Music Collection

## Overview

My Music Collection is a desktop application designed to help Windows users organize and manage their personal music libraries. The software provides a structured way to catalog music files, track metadata, and maintain an accessible database of audio content.

## Key Features

**Organize music files by artist, album, and genre**  
**Edit and update track information including title, year, and composer**  
**Search and filter music collections with customizable criteria**  
**Import and export music data for backup and sharing purposes**  
**Support for a wide range of audio file formats**  
**Automatic detection of duplicate tracks**  
**User-friendly interface optimized for clarity and ease of use**

## Why My Music Collection?

My Music Collection is designed to offer reliable and clear management of music libraries without complexity. It focuses on straightforward usability, allowing users to maintain their collections efficiently. The software provides consistent performance for Windows users, ensuring that music organization tasks are handled with minimal disruption.

## Benefits

My Music Collection helps reduce the time spent searching for specific tracks by providing a well-organized database. Its editing capabilities ensure that users can keep their music metadata accurate and up to date. The application supports stable operation, contributing to a smoother workflow for managing large audio libraries.

## Compatibility

This repository is developed specifically for Windows systems. It delivers stable performance and efficient operation within the Windows environment, making it suitable for personal and professional music management needs.
